## Track 1 — Voice AI
- **`[NEW]` "Real-Time Voice AI Hears but Does Not Listen" (arXiv 2606.26083, Jun 24, Together AI/Stanford).** Tested Gemini 3.1 Flash Live + GPT Realtime 2: both act on transcript content and largely ignore prosody; their accent/age guesses track word-content bias, not acoustics. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26083 — **So what:** direct warning for your stack. Gemini Live may read a Trini clerk's hesitation as a confident yes/no. Design the prompt to *force* an explicit "in stock — yes/no, price, quantity" readback; never infer from tone.
- **`[NEW]` OpenAI ChatGPT dictation STT update (Jun 26).** New STT model, "≥10% lower WER," explicitly improved on *accented English, code-switching, and noisy environments* — exactly your #1 risk. But it shipped **only inside ChatGPT, no API.** — **So what:** not usable yet; signals the accent/noise frontier is moving. Watch for an API release before believing the WER claim — no leaderboard number to cross-check.
- **`[NEW]` AssemblyAI streaming economics (blog, Jun 23 — guidance roundup, not a launch).** Streaming from ~$0.15/hr, sub-300ms, strong entity accuracy on phone/part numbers. https://www.assemblyai.com/blog/best-api-models-for-real-time-speech-recognition-and-transcription — **So what:** a 2-min call's STT leg ≈ $0.005 — negligible. For you, entity accuracy on **part numbers** matters more than headline WER. Worth a Trini-accent A/B vs Gemini Live's native STT.
- **`[NEW]` LiveKit Agents 1.6.3 (Jun 22) & 1.6.4 (Jun 24).** AssemblyAI inference params, a Gemini Realtime tool-rejection fix, turn-detector/STT-handoff fixes. https://github.com/livekit/agents/releases — **So what:** minor. Only relevant if you self-orchestrate a cascade instead of a managed platform; the end-of-turn detector helps avoid talking over a fast clerk.
- **TTS / leaderboards (TTS Arena, HF Open ASR, Artificial Analysis):** nothing newly published in-window. No accent-relevant WER movement to report.
- **Won't survive Trinidad:** prosody-based stock inference (paper above); ChatGPT's better-accent STT (no API). **Cheapest viable path unchanged:** Gemini Live for the speech loop; if Trini-accent quality fails, fall back to a cascade with a ~$0.15/hr streaming STT — the sub-cent STT/TTS legs mean the LLM is what to price.
